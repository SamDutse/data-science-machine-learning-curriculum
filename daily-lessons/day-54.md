## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Train with callbacks
- Plot and read training curves

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Try...Except](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_try_except.asp), [Matplotlib Line](https://www.w3schools.com/python/matplotlib_line.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Compile and train the model
- Callbacks: TensorBoard and early stopping
- Plotting loss and accuracy curves
- Reducing overfitting

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Train with early stopping, plot accuracy and loss curves, and diagnose overfitting.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Submit the training curves with a 3-sentence interpretation.

Save work as `exercises/day-54_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 54: <topic>`.
