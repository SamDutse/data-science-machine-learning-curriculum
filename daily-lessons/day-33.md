**Phase 4:** Milestone Project 1: Heart Disease Classification (Days 31-38)

[Previous: Day 32](day-32.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 34](day-34.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Build a correlation matrix and heatmap
- Separate correlation from causation

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Pandas Correlations](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/pandas_correlations.asp), [Matplotlib Scatter](https://www.w3schools.com/python/matplotlib_scatter.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Age vs maximum heart rate
- Correlation matrix and heatmap
- What patterns suggest and what they do not prove

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Create scatter plots and a correlation heatmap. Discuss what these patterns mean and the danger of over-reading them.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Write a short 'what the data suggests' summary with two charts.

Save work as `exercises/day-33_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 33: <topic>`.
