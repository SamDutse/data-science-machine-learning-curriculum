# Day 57: Communicating your work

**Phase 7:** Communication, Portfolio & Capstone (Days 57-60)

[Previous: Day 56](day-56.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 58](day-58.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Turn a result into a one-page story
- Choose the right chart for the message

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Matplotlib Labels](https://www.w3schools.com/python/matplotlib_labels.asp), [Pandas Plotting](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/pandas_plotting.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Storytelling with data
- Know your audience
- Turning results into clear charts
- Explaining model results to non-technical people
- Presenting to management and stakeholders

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Take one finished project and rework its key findings into a three-chart story for a non-technical audience.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Write a one-page summary of a project for a non-technical manager.

Save work as `exercises/day-57_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 57: <topic>`.
