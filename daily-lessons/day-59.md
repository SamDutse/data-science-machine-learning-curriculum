## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Apply the full workflow independently
- Document decisions as you work

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Modules](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_modules.asp), [File Handling](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_file_handling.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Applying the six-step framework to your own problem
- Choosing and documenting a dataset
- Building end to end

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Work independently on a capstone using a dataset of your choice (preferably local or African data). The tutor acts as reviewer, not instructor.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Finish the capstone notebook and write a draft README.

Save work as `exercises/day-59_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 59: <topic>`.
