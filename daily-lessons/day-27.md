# Day 27: Scikit-learn VII: regression metrics

**Phase 3:** Scikit-learn: Building ML Models (Days 21-30)

[Previous: Day 26](day-26.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 28](day-28.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Compute R2, MAE and MSE
- Pick the right metric for a business problem

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[ML Multiple Regression](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_ml_multiple_regression.asp), [Math](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_math.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- R-squared
- Mean absolute error (MAE)
- Mean squared error (MSE)
- Choosing the right metric via the scoring parameter

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Train a regression model (e.g., house prices) and compare R2, MAE and MSE. Explain which one a business would care about.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Train two regression models and write which one you would deploy and why.

Save work as `exercises/day-27_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 27: <topic>`.
