# Day 17: NumPy II: manipulating arrays

**Phase 2:** Data Science Tools: Environment, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib (Days 11-20)

[Previous: Day 16](day-16.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 18](day-18.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Index, slice and reshape arrays
- Compute basic statistics

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Array Indexing](https://www.w3schools.com/python/numpy/numpy_array_indexing.asp), [Array Slicing](https://www.w3schools.com/python/numpy/numpy_array_slicing.asp), [Array Reshape](https://www.w3schools.com/python/numpy/numpy_array_reshape.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Manipulating arrays: arithmetic and broadcasting
- Aggregation: sum, mean, std, variance
- Reshaping and transposing
- Dot product vs element-wise multiplication

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Slice arrays, reshape and transpose them, and calculate mean/std. Practise the dot product with a small example.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Create a 4x3 sales array and compute the total, mean and best day.

Save work as `exercises/day-17_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 17: <topic>`.
