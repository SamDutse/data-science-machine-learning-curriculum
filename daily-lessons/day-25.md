# Day 25: Scikit-learn V: evaluating models and cross-validation

**Phase 3:** Scikit-learn: Building ML Models (Days 21-30)

[Previous: Day 24](day-24.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 26](day-26.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Evaluate a model with score and cross-validation
- Explain why cross-validation helps

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[ML Train/Test](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_ml_train_test.asp), [ML Cross Validation](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_ml_cross_validation.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- The score() method
- Cross-validation
- Why a single split can mislead
- Overfitting and underfitting revisited

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Compare a single-split score with 5-fold cross-validation scores. Spot signs of overfitting.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Report single-split and cross-validated scores for 3 models in a small table.

Save work as `exercises/day-25_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 25: <topic>`.
