## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Fit a regression model on a time-based split
- Explain why chronological validation matters

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[ML Train/Test](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_ml_train_test.asp), [Classes/Objects](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_classes.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Fitting a Random Forest regressor
- Splitting into training and validation by date
- Why random splits leak information from the future

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Fit a baseline model on a small sample. Create train and validation sets by date.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Record baseline training and validation scores; comment on the gap.

Save work as `exercises/day-43_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 43: <topic>`.
