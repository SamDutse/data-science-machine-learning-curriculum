# Day 13: Pandas I: Series, DataFrames and CSVs

**Phase 2:** Data Science Tools: Environment, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib (Days 11-20)

[Previous: Day 12](day-12.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 14](day-14.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Create a DataFrame
- Load a CSV and describe it

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[Pandas Intro](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/pandas_intro.asp), [Pandas Series](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/pandas_series.asp), [Pandas DataFrames](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/pandas_dataframes.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Pandas introduction
- Series and DataFrames
- Importing and exporting CSV files, reading data from URLs
- Describing data (shape, dtypes, describe)

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Create Series and DataFrames by hand, then load a CSV from ZTM's course materials or a Nigerian open dataset and inspect it.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Load any CSV, then report its shape, column types and the 3 most interesting columns.

Save work as `exercises/day-13_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 13: <topic>`.
