# Day 55: Project 3: predictions, saving and custom images

**Phase 6:** Data Engineering, Deep Learning & Dog Vision (Days 47-56)

[Previous: Day 54](day-54.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 56](day-56.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Predict on new images
- Save and reload a trained model

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[File Handling](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_file_handling.asp), [JSON](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_json.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Making predictions and decoding labels
- Visualising predictions
- Saving and loading the model
- Predicting on your own images

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Visualise predictions with confidence, save the model, reload it, and predict on photos you took yourself.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Test the model on 5 of your own photos and report what works and what fails.

Save work as `exercises/day-55_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 55: <topic>`.
