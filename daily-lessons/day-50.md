# Day 50: Building your first Keras model

**Phase 6:** Data Engineering, Deep Learning & Dog Vision (Days 47-56)

[Previous: Day 49](day-49.md) | [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) | [Next: Day 51](day-51.md)

## Learning objectives

By the end of today the student can:

- Build, compile and train a small model
- Read training curves

## 1. Before class (30-60 min, independent)

**Python (W3Schools): click and study**

[List Comprehension](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_lists_comprehension.asp), [Iterators](https://www.w3schools.com/python/python_iterators.asp)

**ZTM video topics to watch (open in your offline copy)**

- Building a model with Keras Sequential
- Compile: loss, optimiser, metrics
- Fit and evaluate
- Over- and underfitting in neural networks

Write down at least two questions and try every example yourself.

## 2. In class (60-120 min, guided practice)

Build a small Sequential model on a simple dataset, train it, and plot its loss curve.

The tutor asks the student to explain each decision out loud before moving on.

## 3. After class (30-60 min, independent)

**Homework:** Train a small model twice with different settings and compare loss curves.

Save work as `exercises/day-50_<topic>.ipynb` (or `.py`) in the student's own repo and commit it with a message like `Day 50: <topic>`.
